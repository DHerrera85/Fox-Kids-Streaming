Top Fox Kids Latin America Blocks

Published on enmemoriapokesog.blogspot.com on May 4, 2012

Anime Invasion Block

One of Fox Kids' most successful blocks was undoubtedly Anime Invasion, a lineup entirely dedicated to Japanese animation. It drew massive audiences and remained on the air even after the rebrand to Jetix, featuring titles like Sonic X, Megaman NT Warrior, Pucca, and Battle B-Daman. By 2007, in Jetix’s final late-night anime hours, reruns of Shaman King, Medabots Damashii, Ultimate Muscle, and B-Daman marked the end of an era. The block had officially been discontinued a few months after the Jetix transition.

Featured Shows:
Flint the Time Detective, Shinzo, Monster Rancher, Track City, Dinozaurs: The Series, Patlabor, Digimon (all seasons), Crayon Shin-Chan, Medabots, Escaflowne, Shaman King, Heavy Gear, Beyblade, Mon Colle Knights, Kirby, Transformers Armada, Ultimate Muscle, Megaman NT Warrior.

Mysteria Block

In mid-2001, Fox Kids debuted Scary... But True, a mystery show that received positive feedback. Once it ended, the channel doubled down on the spooky theme with the launch of Mysteria in 2002.

This late-night block, which aired daily starting at 9 p.m., became one of the key transitions into the Jetix era. Each evening began with an hour of Goosebumps followed by other suspenseful series.

Mysteria lasted for several years, and its final addition was Dark Oracle, which continued to air on Jetix until 2007. The block officially ended on June 6, 2006, during its final late-night reruns.

Featured Shows: Goosebumps, Strange Days at Blake Holsey High, Spooky Sisters, Wicked Science, So Weird, Dark Oracle.

“Super Chiflados” Block

Running from late 2000 to early 2002, this morning and early afternoon comedy block showcased zany animated shows. The only anime included was Super Pig (Tonde Burin), which didn’t air under the anime block due to its lighthearted tone. Early episodes of Angela Anaconda were also part of the mix before the block was eventually phased out.

Featured Shows: The New Woody Woodpecker Show, Oggy and the Cockroaches, Megababies, Tonde Burin, Angela Anaconda, Pigs Next Door, Bobby’s World, Kids from Room 402.

Insomnia Block (Insomnio)

Originally named Insomnia in 2001, it was later stylized as Insomnio and aired until 2003. This late-night block featured a mix of classic cartoons and retro series, including some beloved by previous generations.

It also included the debut of Moville Mysteries and reruns of cult shows like Mortadelo & Filemón. The aim was to appeal to older viewers and nostalgic fans.

Featured Shows: Moville Mysteries, The Zorro (1957), Mortadelo y Filemón, The Addams Family (1964–1966), Spider-Man (1994), Twin Sitters, Dilbert, The Incredible Hulk (1996), Shaman King (re-airings), The Munsters, Laurel & Hardy, Batman (1966–1968), The Three Stooges, Mission Hill.

Girl Power Block

Aired mid-morning from 2001 to 2002, Girl Power was specifically designed to attract young female audiences. The lineup included empowering shows and cartoons starring strong female leads. While the block was eventually dissolved, many of its titles continued airing in other time slots.

Featured Shows: Angela Anaconda, What About Mimi, Braceface, Tonde Burin, Wheel Squad, Totally Spies!, Mary-Kate and Ashley in Action!

Midnight Classics Block

This short-lived 2001 programming block aired on weeknights after midnight and showcased vintage animated series from earlier decades. It struggled with low ratings and was later replaced by Insomnia, which offered a broader selection of retro and concluded shows.

Featured Shows: Spider-Man (1967), The Marvel Super Heroes (1966), Woody Woodpecker (1957), Spider-Man and His Amazing Friends, Iron Man (1966), Spiderwoman (1979), The Incredible Hulk (1966).

Mini Fox Blox / Itsy Bitsy Time

Initially called Mini Fox Blox, this preschool-focused block aired from late 2000 to mid-2001, running daily at 8 a.m. for two hours. Much like Nick Jr., it offered safe and entertaining content for younger viewers.

Featured Shows: Charley & Mimmo, Tom and Vicky, The Animal Shelf, Itsy Bitsy Time (animated shorts), 64 Zoo Lane, Budgie the Little Helicopter, ZZZap!
